Our History

*

2018

The band is formed in a garage in downtown Shanghai, blending traditional Chinese music with hard rock.

*

2019

First live gig at The Dragon's Den, receiving local acclaim for their unique sound.

*

2020

Released debut EP "Spicy Riffs", gaining international attention on streaming platforms.

*

2022

Embarked on their first Asia tour, "Wok & Roll", playing sold-out shows in Tokyo, Seoul, and Bangkok.
